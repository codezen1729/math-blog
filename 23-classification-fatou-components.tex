\section*{Classification of Non-Wandering Fatou Components}
% BLOG-CONTENT-BEGIN

Let $f$ be a rational map on the Riemann sphere $\hat{\mathbb{C}}$ with $\deg(f)\geq2$. This post reviews the background needed to classify the periodic Fatou components of $f$ up to conformal equivalence \cite{milnor2011dynamics}.

\subsection{Dynamics on Hyperbolic Surfaces}

Since the Julia set is non-empty and has no isolated points, the Fatou components are \BlogPost{hyperbolic-geometry}{hyperbolic}. One can now study periodic Fatou components by replacing $f$ with a suitable iterate and procuring information from dynamics on hyperbolic surfaces. Classifying dynamics on hyperbolic surfaces in itself is not interesting because of the sheer lack of chaotic behaviour; we nevertheless use the classification as a tool for studying Fatou components.

\subsection*{Theorem 2.29 (Classification of Dynamics on Hyperbolic Surfaces)}
Let $h:S\to S$ be a holomorphic self-map of a hyperbolic Riemann surface. Exactly one of the following possibilities holds:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Every orbit converges to a unique attracting fixed point of $h$.
    \item Every orbit diverges to infinity with respect to the Poincar\'e metric on $S$.
    \item $h$ is an automorphism of finite order; that is, $h^{\circ n}=\operatorname{Id}_S$ for some $n\geq1$.
    \item $S$ is conformally isomorphic to $\mathbb{D}$, $\mathbb{D}\backslash\{0\}$, or $\mathbb{A}_r:=\{z\mid1<|z|<r\}$, and in these coordinates $h(z)=e^{2\pi i\alpha}z$ for some $\alpha\in\mathbb{R}\backslash\mathbb{Q}$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Fatou Components}

The image of a Fatou component $U$ under $f$ is also a Fatou component. A Fatou component $U$ can do one of the following four things:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $f(U) = U$ (invariant Fatou component).
    \item $f^{\circ p}(U)=U$ for a least integer $p\geq2$ (periodic but not invariant Fatou component).
    \item $U$ is not periodic, but $f^{\circ m}(U)$ is periodic for some $m\geq1$ (strictly preperiodic Fatou component).
    \item $f^{\circ m}(U)\neq f^{\circ n}(U)$ whenever $m\neq n$ (wandering Fatou component).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Theorem 2.48 (Classification of Periodic Fatou Components)}
Let $U$ be a periodic Fatou component of exact period $p$, and set $F=f^{\circ p}$. Exactly one of the following holds:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $U$ is the immediate basin of an attracting fixed point of $F$.
    \item $U$ is the immediate basin of one attracting petal of a parabolic fixed point of $F$.
    \item $(U,F)$ is conformally conjugate to an irrational rotation of $\mathbb{D}$; thus $U$ is a Siegel disk.
    \item $(U,F)$ is conformally conjugate to an irrational rotation of an annulus; thus $U$ is a Herman ring.
\end{enumerate}

If $U$ is strictly preperiodic, then $f^{\circ m}:U\to f^{\circ m}(U)$ is a proper holomorphic map of finite degree onto a periodic component for some $m\geq1$.
